# Enterprise overview

The portfolio implements a research-first Sovereign Evidence Architecture.

## Layer 1 — Research

The four repositories own their estimands, experiments, numerical methods, and scientific interpretations. Contract enforcement should not be embedded throughout research-essential code.

## Layer 2 — Qualification

Repository adapters declare the evidence fields and qualifications owed by each native result. The monotone qualification algebra derives publication status; adapters do not independently assert the final status.

## Layer 3 — Sovereignty and privacy

Controlled release requires declared ownership, provenance, disclosure class, purpose, approvals, and a trust decision bound to the exact canonical payload and policy version.

## Layer 4 — Release services

Figure registries, manifests, signatures, policy migration records, and portfolio verification are release services. They support reproducibility and auditability without dominating the scientific surface.

## Audience paths

- Researchers: `repositories/`
- Research engineers: `framework/qualification/`
- Enterprise architects and governance teams: `architecture/`
- Release and audit operators: `tools/` and `manifests/`
